# Research agent reports behind the 2026-09-11 Apify evaluation

Three Sonnet research agents ran read-only on 2026-09-11, one each on Apify's Instagram
actors, Apify's TikTok actors plus TikTok's official APIs, and the official Instagram
route plus the legal picture. Their full reports are kept here verbatim as the sources
for `../2026-09-11-apify-instagram-tiktok.md`. Nothing in them was run on Apify. Facts
the evaluation relies on were re-read in the main session and are marked SPOT-CHECKED
there; everything else in these files is the agent's own reading, with the URL it cited.
